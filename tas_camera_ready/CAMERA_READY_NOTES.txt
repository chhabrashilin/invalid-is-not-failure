What changed relative to the reviewed version
---------------------------------------------
R1.1, R3.4  Finite-corpus estimand p = N^-1 sum Y_i; N used throughout.
R1.2, R3.2  Mean horizon gap replaced by the survival ratio rho = pbar1/pbar0
            (Prop. 2, Table 1); mean gap shown to be first order only; stochastic
            dominance checked (fails in both corpora, yet rho > 1 for q <= 5%).
R1.3, R3.1  Opposite-direction claim conditioned on rho > 1; 5 of 29 tau-bench
            models have rho < 1, where both policies err downward.
R1.4        Invalid-as-failure is conservative for levels, not comparisons.
R1.5, R3.3  Definition 1 (validity rule); context exhaustion is a valid failure.
R1.6, R1.7, R3.5  Abstract and results framed as hypothetical hazards; simulation
            agreement described as a code check, not validation.
R1.8        Prop. 3 (rerun until valid): removes 70-72% of SWE-agent's bias.
R1.9        Early abort described as outcome-dependent, outside the model.
R1.10       Appendix A.6 now holds the misspecified-hazard results, with corpus
            (SWE-agent) and hazards (0.5%, 1%) stated.
R4.1        Bounded non-binary scores covered after Prop. 1.
R4.2        Prop. 4: Kaplan-Meier weighting needs only observed quantities;
            validated under constant and step-dependent hazards; observed
            container failures (early_exit) analysed as real censoring.
