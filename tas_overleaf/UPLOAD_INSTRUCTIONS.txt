OVERLEAF UPLOAD -- TAS 2026 anonymous short paper
=================================================

1. Download the official AAAI-26 author kit:
      https://aaai.org/authorkit26/
   (AAAI-27 kit also accepted by TAS; if you use it, change the two
   "aaai26" strings in main.tex to "aaai27".)

2. Overleaf -> New Project -> Upload Project -> upload the AAAI author kit ZIP.
   This gives you aaai26.sty and aaai26.bst in the project root.

3. Into that same project, upload these two files from tas_overleaf/,
   overwriting the kit's sample files if prompted:
      main.tex
      references.bib

4. Menu -> Compiler: pdfLaTeX.  Menu -> Main document: main.tex.
   Click Recompile. Overleaf runs bibtex automatically; if references show
   as [?], hit Recompile once more.

5. ONLY IF it fails with an error about \affiliations:
   open main.tex and uncomment the line   %\affiliations{}   (just below
   \author{Anonymous Submission}). Recompile.

6. Download PDF and submit to EasyChair.

NOTES
-----
- No figure files are needed. Figure 1 is drawn inline with TikZ.
- Do not add \usepackage{geometry}, fullpage, hyperref, titlesec or caption;
  the AAAI style rejects them.
- Keep letterpaper. Do not add page numbers.
- The paper is anonymous: no authors, affiliations, acknowledgements,
  repository URLs, or institution names appear anywhere in the source.

PACKAGES REQUIRED (all standard in Overleaf's TeX Live)
-------------------------------------------------------
  aaai26     -- from the official author kit (NOT on CTAN; you must upload it)
  times, helvet, courier
  url, graphicx, natbib
  booktabs
  tikz  + libraries: arrows.meta, positioning
